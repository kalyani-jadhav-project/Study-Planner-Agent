# 🎓 StudyGenie — AI Study Planner Agent

**An AI-powered college study planner that helps students organize subjects, prepare for exams, and manage their daily study schedules efficiently.**

## 📌 About the Project

StudyGenie is an AI-powered study planning application designed to help college students organize their academic activities and prepare for examinations more effectively.

Students can add subjects, manage syllabus topics, set exam dates, specify difficulty levels, and define their available daily study hours. Using AI integration with the Gemini API, the application helps generate personalized study plans based on students' academic requirements.

The application provides an interactive dashboard, a personalized study timetable, and subject management features to make study planning easier and more organized.

## ✨ Key Features

- 🤖 **AI-Powered Study Planning:** Generate personalized study plans using the Gemini API.
- 📊 **Academic Dashboard:** Track study progress, active tasks, study hours, and upcoming exams.
- 📅 **Personalized Timetable:** Organize daily study sessions according to subjects, priorities, and available time.
- 📚 **Subject Management:** Add subjects, syllabus topics, exam dates, and difficulty levels.
- ⏰ **Time Management:** Allocate study hours based on daily availability.
- ✅ **Task Tracking:** Mark study sessions as completed, pending, or missed.
- 🎯 **Exam Preparation:** Monitor upcoming exams and organize preparation according to deadlines.

## 🖥️ Application Screenshots

### 1. Academic Dashboard

The Academic Dashboard provides a quick overview of the student's study progress and academic activities.

It displays:
- **Completion Pace:** Shows the percentage of study tasks completed.
- **Study Hours:** Tracks study time against the available daily study limit.
- **Active Tasks:** Displays the number of pending and missed tasks.
- **Upcoming Exams:** Shows the countdown to the next examination.
- **Subject Completion Breakdown:** Provides subject-wise progress information.

This dashboard helps students understand their current preparation status and identify the tasks that need attention.

![StudyGenie Academic Dashboard](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6ad26811-1b0a-4c4b-8956-7521fe4b72c1)

### 2. Personalized Study Timetable

The Personalized Study Timetable organizes study tasks into structured sessions for different dates and subjects.

It includes:
- **Daily Study Sessions:** Displays the topics students should study each day.
- **Priority Levels:** Highlights tasks according to their importance.
- **Time Allocation:** Shows the estimated duration of each study session.
- **Task Status:** Allows students to mark tasks as Done, Pending, or Missed.
- **Daily Study Budget:** Helps keep planned study time within the configured daily limit.
- **Session Filtering:** Allows students to view all sessions, today's sessions, pending tasks, missed tasks, and completed tasks.

This feature helps students follow a structured routine and manage their exam preparation more effectively.

![StudyGenie Personalized Study Timetable](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/face856f-d3ca-442f-bc0b-1c188686c7ad)

### 3. Subjects and Topics Management

The Subjects & Topics section allows students to configure their academic information before generating a personalized study plan.

Students can:
- Add subjects and syllabus topics.
- Set examination dates.
- Choose difficulty levels such as Easy, Medium, or Hard.
- Set daily available study hours.
- View registered subjects and their topics.
- Load sample college data for demonstration, where supported.

The subject information helps the application organize study sessions according to the student's syllabus, difficulty levels, and exam deadlines.

## 🔄 How It Works

1. **Add Subjects:** Students register their subjects and syllabus topics.
2. **Set Exam Details:** Students specify exam dates, subject difficulty, and available daily study hours.
3. **Generate an AI Plan:** The application uses the provided academic information to help generate a personalized study plan.
4. **Organize the Timetable:** Study tasks are arranged into daily sessions with priorities and estimated durations.
5. **Track Progress:** Students update task statuses and monitor their academic progress through the dashboard.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of StudyGenie is to simplify academic planning by helping students organize their syllabus, prioritize subjects, allocate study time, and monitor exam preparation in one place.

- The `.env` file should be excluded using `.gitignore`.
- No database is required if the application is designed to manage study information without persistent database storage.
